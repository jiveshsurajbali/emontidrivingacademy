Learning How to Drive

The Basics of Vehicle Operation
We begin with a refresh of the basic controls of a car, as covered in the learner's test
Starting Maneuvers
Get comfortable operating the vehicle in a controlled space, by practicing take off, maneuvering, gear-shifting and stopping within a closed test yard.
The Yard Test
Yard tests include practice runs of the pre-trip inspection, alley docking, parallel parking and three-pont turns.
Take to the Streets
You then take the big step to the streets! We go out onto the test route for your desired Traffic Department's test.
Take on Traffic
As you improve in confidence, road test lessons move from quiet hours to busier periods to get you comfortable around other cars.
The Full Driving Experience
Before you know it, you will be ready for the open road. To get comfortable, you will be driving to the test yard from your pickup point.
Test Day
Finally, we arrive at the test day. We like to give the students one last practice run before the test, then it's all up to you!

Test Day Tips

  • Be sure to refer back to your learner's notes. The information contained within is vital for your test.
  • Take as many practice lessons as you need, its best to be over-prepared rather than under-prepared.
  • Make sure to pay attention to your own habits,as well as the instructors notes. It is iron out weak ares before the test.
  • Make sure to get a good nights sleep, food and hydration before the test. You need to be as alert as possible.
  • We recommend booking your test as early as possible, to avoid stress and over-thinking throughout the day.
  • Try your best to relax before the test, avoid any unnecessary stressors. Spend time on activities you know will keep you calm.
  • Leave for the test station as early as possible to avoid delays and guarantee punctuality. You don't want to miss your test!
  • Be friendly and respectful of all staff. Remember that if they mark you down, they're just doing their job.
  • Failing is not the end of the world. Many have failed and succeed in later attempts. You can always take the test again!

Testing Routes

We serve the East London area and surrounds. Take your pick from the following testing stations:

  • East London
  • Gonubie
  • Mdantsane
  • Wilsonia

For the busiest and most difficult test environment, sign up for your test at the East London Traffic Department where you will be put through the paces in the the busiest road in East London: Oxford Street. Likewise, Mdantsane will put you in a high pressure scenario as you navigate the busy streets.

The Wilsonia test route will put you in a slightly quieter route, however the industrial nature of the area means dealing with the challenges of driving around large trucks.

For the easiest and most stress-free test, Gonubie navigates through quiet residential avenues, a mildly busy main road and a quiet seaside route. Whether your goal is to test yourself in the most challenging environment or to make your test as easy as possible, we can accommodate you.
